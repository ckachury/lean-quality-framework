# GQM canvas

Fill one canvas per business goal. Keep it to one page.

```
GOAL (business)        ___________________________________________
Owner                  ___________   Horizon  ___________

QUESTION (quality)     What do we need to know about product quality
                       to reach this goal?
                       1. ________________________________________
                       2. ________________________________________

METRIC                 Name | Definition | Source | Baseline | Target | Reviewed in
                       ____ | __________ | ______ | ________ | ______ | __________

DECISION               Which decision will this metric change?
                       ___________________________________________
```

## Worked examples

| Goal | Question | Metric | Decision it informs |
|---|---|---|---|
| +15% first-month retention | Are onboarding bugs driving users away? | Critical errors reported in the first 24 hours; onboarding drop-off by step | Which onboarding step gets fixed or tested first |
| Raise conversion from trial to paid | Does checkout fail or feel slow? | Payment errors per 1,000 attempts; p95 checkout time | Whether to delay a release that touches payments |
| Close a funding round | Can we show the product is reliable? | Incidents per month by severity; time to restore | What reliability story goes into the investor deck |
| Ship faster without breaking things | Are releases slowed down by fear? | Lead time for changes; share of releases rolled back | Where to invest in automation next |
